Domain Mail Organizer

Mail accounts

Setup reuses folders with the configured domain-root and archive names or creates them when missing, then imports direct child folders as customer rules. Automatic moves never cross accounts.

Mail processing

Set matching defaults, process an entire Inbox in review batches, or recover customer mail from the Archive.

Processing defaults

Mailbox tools

These tools use your saved customer rules and always open a review before moving mail.

Enable a mail account and add at least one customer before using these tools.

Customer rules

Customers open collapsed and sorted by name. Expand a customer to edit its rules. Domain matching is exact: shutterfly.com does not match em.shutterfly.com. Add each domain or subdomain you want to match, and never enter a public suffix such as co.uk.
